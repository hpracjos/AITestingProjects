🚀 Want to get noticed by top tech recruiters? Your LinkedIn profile needs to be an "All-Star"! ⭐

If you're a QA, SDET, or Software Engineer looking for your next big role, an optimized profile is non-negotiable. 

Here are the 7 REQUIRED sections you must complete first to achieve All-Star status (and hack the LinkedIn algorithm):

1️⃣ Profile Photo 📸
A professional headshot makes you 11x more likely to be found by hiring managers! Skip the selfies; use a clean, professional image.

2️⃣ Headline 🎯
Don't just put your job title. Highlight your skills and impact! Example: 
"SDET Manager | AI & LLM Test Architect | Playwright • Selenium | Helping QA Engineers Thrive in the AI Era"

3️⃣ About / Summary ✍️
Tell your story! Use a compelling summary (around 150 words) that highlights your journey, achievements, and what you're building.

4️⃣ Experience 💼
Add your roles (at least 3!) with detailed descriptions of your responsibilities and achievements. Don't just list tasks—highlight outcomes!

5️⃣ Education 🎓
Don't forget to include your degree and university. It's a key search filter for recruiters.

6️⃣ Skills 🛠️
You need at least 5 skills listed. For QAs, prioritize things like: Playwright, Selenium, Test Automation, Python, Java, AI Testing, API Testing, and CI/CD.

7️⃣ Connections 🤝
You need at least 50 connections! Sync your contacts and connect with former colleagues.
