# AI Image Prompts for LinkedIn & Medium Posts

Use these prompts in tools like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion to generate an engaging cover image for your posts.

## Option 1: The Modern Tech Professional (Conceptual)
**Prompt:**
> A high-quality, modern 3D illustration of a glowing, golden star badge with the words "ALL STAR" subtly engraved. The star is floating above a sleek, glowing digital LinkedIn profile interface on a transparent glass screen. In the background, subtle tech elements like floating code snippets (Python, Java), testing gears, and connecting nodes. Color palette: Professional LinkedIn blue, vibrant gold, and dark mode tech background. Lighting: Cinematic, soft neon glow. Style: Isometric 3D, premium, highly detailed.

## Option 2: The QA/SDET Career Journey
**Prompt:**
> A vibrant, modern illustration of a confident software testing professional (QA/SDET) sitting at a sleek desk with multiple monitors showing code and automated test scripts (Playwright, Selenium). Above them, 7 glowing steps leading upward towards a bright, glowing star. The scene represents career growth and profile optimization. Flat vector art style mixed with subtle gradients, corporate tech aesthetic, clean lines, utilizing shades of professional blue, teal, and gold. 

## Option 3: Minimalist Typography & Icons
**Prompt:**
> A clean, minimalist flat lay design on a dark navy blue background. In the center, 7 glowing neon icons arranged in a circle: a camera (Profile Photo), a megaphone (Headline), a document (About), a briefcase (Experience), a graduation cap (Education), a wrench/gear (Skills), and people connecting (Connections). In the middle of the circle, a large, glowing gold star. Text "All-Star Profile" integrated smoothly. Tech-focused, professional, eye-catching for social media feeds.
